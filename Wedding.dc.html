Recommended Arrival

Fly into Querétaro

QRO is the easiest arrival. Most U.S. routes connect through Dallas/Fort Worth, then it is about one hour by car to San Miguel. Check León/Bajío (BJX) only when the schedule is clearly better.

Los Angeles

LAX OR TIJ / QRO OR BJX

For the budget route, train or drive to CBX, cross into TIJ, and fly nonstop to QRO or BJX. Often the simplest option.

Detroit

DTW / QRO OR BJX

American through Dallas/Fort Worth or United through Houston. QRO first; BJX as the alternate.

Want Time in Mexico City?

Fly into MEX, stay one or two nights, then drive to San Miguel. The transfer is three-plus hours, so choose it for the city, not convenience.

Reserve your ride. QRO is about an hour from San Miguel. Arrange a driver.

03

Getting Around

San Miguel is made for walking. The historic center is compact and easy to explore on foot. The streets are cobblestone and the hills are real, so bring comfortable shoes.

Use a taxi or Uber for the climb. Rent a car only if you are planning day trips beyond San Miguel. All wedding events are within a couple of walkable blocks.
