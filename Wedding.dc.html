Recommended Arrival

Fly into Querétaro

QRO is our primary recommendation. It is about a 1-hour drive to San Miguel, with straightforward connections through DFW or IAH. Volaris offers limited direct flights from Los Angeles to QRO on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Sundays.

MEX

Mexico City

Spend a few days exploring Mexico City before the wedding. From there, take a 30-minute flight to Querétaro, followed by a 1-hour drive to San Miguel. You can also drive directly from Mexico City in about 4 hours.

Travel Tips

  • Check your passport expiration date. Six months of validity beyond your return date is recommended.
  • For those wanting to spend a couple of days in Mexico City before or after the wedding, it is about a 30-minute flight from QRO or a four hour drive from San Miguel.
  • To attend the full weekend, plan to travel Thursday through Sunday.

Airport & general transportation. Email Maritza at contact@tripsanmiguel.com with your flight information to request a price. SUVs and vans are available. You can also book through TransportArte Mexico or ask your hotel to arrange a car.

🐝︎

Getting Around

Stay in Centro, the heart of San Miguel. Choose accommodations within an easy walk of the wedding venues, and check the walking route before booking. The streets are cobblestone and the hills are real, so bring comfortable shoes.

Use a taxi or Uber for the climb. Rent a car only if you are planning day trips beyond San Miguel. All wedding events are within a couple of walkable blocks.
